---
title: Suppressions
description: Automatic protection for your sender reputation.
---

The suppression list protects your sender reputation — and your SES account —
by making sure you never repeatedly mail an address that hard-bounced or
marked you as spam.

## How addresses get suppressed

- **Hard bounce** — the receiving server permanently rejected the address.
- **Complaint** — the recipient marked a message as spam.

Both arrive as SES events and suppress the address automatically. Suppressions
are per team.

## What suppression does

- **Transactional sends** (`POST /emails`, SMTP relay): suppressed recipients
  are stripped from `to`/`cc`/`bcc`. If *every* `to` recipient is suppressed,
  the send is rejected with `422 All recipients are suppressed`.
- **Broadcasts**: suppressed contacts are skipped during fan-out.

## Reviewing and removing

The dashboard lists every suppressed address with the reason and date. You can
remove an address to allow sending again — do this only when you know the
cause is fixed (e.g. a mailbox that existed all along but was rejected by a
misconfigured server). Re-suppression is automatic on the next bounce or
complaint.

## Why this matters

SES tracks account-level bounce and complaint rates and pauses accounts that
cross its thresholds. MillionSend's metrics page tracks your rates against
those thresholds, and [broadcast sending](/concepts/broadcasts#guardrails) is
blocked automatically when a rate crosses the pause line.
